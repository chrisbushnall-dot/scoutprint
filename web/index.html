Normalize

All action coordinates use a 0–100 pitch with the attacking goal at x=100. A 16×12 grid becomes a unit-mass probability distribution.

This Vercel build blends cosine overlap, Jensen–Shannon shape similarity and projected Earth Mover distance. The VPS research backend uses exact shortlisted Sinkhorn divergence.
